Canon · branching directions

Ten ways to branch a tomato sauce

One base in every version — tomato, garlic, onion and basil softened in olive oil — and the same six additions that carry it somewhere with its own name. Nothing peels off backwards from a descending spine, which was the problem last time. Note that three of these — fan, arc and orbit — do spread symmetrically, so half of each spread sits left of the base; the other seven grow strictly down or to the right.

01

Fan

Base on top, everything it becomes in one row underneath. The flattest reading — all six are equally far from the base, which is true.

02

Bus

Base at the left, one vertical rail, dishes off to the right. Scales past six without getting wider — the one that survives a sauce with twenty children.

03

Arc

The fan bent into a curve so the outer dishes sit closer to the base. Prettier; costs you the tidy alignment of a row.

04

Staircase

The only one that admits some sauces are on the way to others — vodka really is rosa with vodka and pancetta added, so it hangs off rosa rather than off the base.

05

Tiers

Three bands: what you start with, what you add, what you get. No arcs at all — the alignment does the connecting.

06

Grouped

Two levels: the kind of thing you added, then the sauce. Says something the others can’t — that rosa and vodka are the same move, made twice.

07

Orbit

Base in the middle, everything at equal distance around the lower half. No hierarchy implied and no lines crossing — but it eats the most space per sauce.

08

Accumulating chips

Shows the base carried into every row so you can see it is still there. The one that makes “everything else is the same” visible rather than stated.

tomatogarliconionbasil + cream→Rosa
tomatogarliconionbasil + cream+ vodka+ pancetta→Vodka
tomatogarliconionbasil + guanciale+ pecorino→Amatriciana
tomatogarliconionbasil + dried chilli→Arrabbiata
tomatogarliconionbasil + olives+ capers+ anchovy→Puttanesca
tomatogarliconionbasil + eggplant+ ricotta salata→alla Norma
09

Outline

No drawing at all — just indentation, the way a file tree works. Costs nothing to render, reads fine on a phone, and nests as deep as you like.

Tomato · garlic · onion · basil, softened in olive oil ├─ + cream Rosa │ └─ + vodka, pancetta Vodka ├─ + guanciale, pecorino Amatriciana ├─ + dried chilli Arrabbiata ├─ + olives, capers, anchovy Puttanesca └─ + fried eggplant, ricotta salata alla Norma

10

Metro

The base runs left to right as a line you stay on; each addition is a stop where a branch peels away and terminates. Reads as a journey rather than a hierarchy.
