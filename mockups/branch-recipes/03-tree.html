Variation 01 — Cinnamon-Maple

branches at step 03 · 3 changes
Cinnamon-Maple

35m · serves 8 · easy

branch ingredient — maple syrup

The one everyone reaches for. Maple stands in for the granulated sugar, so the coating sets glassy instead of sandy — and it needs a thinner spread, because maple travels further than sugar does.

Changes from the base

+ add   − drop   → swap
+Maple Syrup (grade B)1/4 cup
+Ground Cinnamon2 tsp
−Granulated Sugar1/3 cup

Method — replaces step 03

Toss with the cinnamon, salt and maple in place of the sugar and butter. Spread thinner than you think you should; maple pools, and any puddle stays chewy after the nuts have gone crisp.

Variation 02 — Lavender-Coconut

branches at step 04 · 3 changes
Lavender-Coconut

35m · serves 8 · medium

branch ingredient — dried lavender

Floral and faintly resinous. Grind the lavender into the sugar first or it reads like soap; the coconut goes on late, because it browns long before the nuts are done.

Changes from the base

+ add   − drop   → swap
+Dried Culinary Lavender (ground with the sugar)1 tsp
+Unsweetened Coconut Flakes1/2 cup
+Lemon Zest1 tsp

Method — inserted inside step 04

Scatter the coconut over the pan for the final 8 minutes only, then pull the sheet the moment the flakes go the colour of straw. Zest over the top while everything is still hot.

Variation 03 — Rosemary-Black Pepper

branches at step 03 · 4 changes
Rosemary-Black Pepper

35m · serves 8 · easy

branch ingredient — fresh rosemary

The savoury branch, and the one that stops the bowl disappearing before dinner. Sugar drops to a token amount, salt doubles, and the rosemary goes on off the heat so it stays green rather than dusty.

Changes from the base

+ add   − drop   → swap
+Fresh Rosemary (finely chopped)2 tbsp
+Black Pepper (coarse)1 tsp
→Granulated Sugar1/3 cup → 2 tbsp
→Kosher Salt1 1/2 tsp → 1 tbsp

Method — replaces step 03, extends step 05

Toss with the reduced sugar, the doubled salt, the pepper and the butter. Bake as written, then throw the rosemary through the moment the pan comes out — residual heat is enough to open it up without frying it brown.

Variation 04 — Chili-Lime

branches at step 05 · 4 changes
Chili-Lime

35m · serves 8 · easy

branch ingredient — chipotle chili

The loud one, and the latest branch on the tree — nothing changes until the pan is out of the oven. Lime goes on cold, or the acid steams the lacquer soft within the hour.

Changes from the base

+ add   − drop   → swap
+Chipotle Chili Powder2 tsp
+Lime Zest (added cold)2 limes
+Flaky Salt (to finish)to taste
−Unsalted Butter1 tbsp

Method — added after step 05

Stir the chili powder in with the sugar before baking. Once the nuts are stone cold, zest the limes straight over the pan, add flaky salt, and toss with your hands so nothing clumps.

how it behaves

  • The tree sits where the flavour panel normally sits — the one hairline box the system allows — and it is the only new surface the concept introduces.
  • Clicking any node scrolls to its section and inks the path from the base out to that node; every other branch stays at hairline grey.
  • Scrolling updates the tree on its own. The condensed rail under the panel is sticky, so the structure never leaves the screen while you read a branch.
  • Variations are sections, not separate recipes: each one lists only its delta from the base (add, drop, swap) plus the step it replaces or extends.
  • The dashed node at the right end of the spine is the affordance for adding a fifth branch — the diagram doubles as the editor's entry point.
