Tabbed variations

One page, one title, one hero. The variations live in a tab strip directly under the head — the Navigation nav-link idiom (1px hairline baseline, black rule under the active item), borrowed downward. Switching a tab re-renders only the ingredient and method lines that differ; everything else is inherited from Base and stays put. Changed lines are marked with a hairline rule in the left gutter and a micro tag, never with colour.

A — on the recipe page /recipes/spiced-nuts

Try it: switch tabs. The head, hero, lede, nutrition and the changed lines swap; inherited lines do not move. Tap a step to check it off, or turn the change marks off to read the variation as if it were its own recipe.
