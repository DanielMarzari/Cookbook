Diff view

A branched recipe is not five recipes. It is one recipe plus a short list of overrides. This concept prints the base in full and sets each variation's overrides in place — shared lines stay in normal ink, changed lines get a thin left rule and the variation's name in small caps. Pick a variation and you still read the base; what you see marked is only the delta.

Section A

On the recipe page

Switch variations in the head. The page never reloads into a different recipe — the same lines stay put and the deltas slot in.

the switch

Variations sit under the meta line as plain underlined text, the same affordance as a filter. "all four" stacks every branch at once.

the read-out

One hairline panel counts the delta and ticks it against the 16 lines of the base. Three ink ticks out of sixteen is the whole argument.

collapse

"collapse unchanged lines" folds everything a variation leaves alone, keeping the original step numbers. The recipe shrinks to its diff.

Spiced Nuts

55m  ·  serves 8  ·  easy  ·  approved  ·  via Family Cookbook

Egg-white glazed pecans, almonds and cashews baked low so the coating sets glassy rather than sticky. The base is deliberately plain — it is the trunk the other four grow off.

Method — tap a step to check it off

7 steps
01

Heat the oven to 300°F and line a sheet pan with parchment.

02

Whisk the egg white with the water until loose and foamy, about 30 seconds — you want it slack, not meringue.

03

Toss the nuts in the egg white until every surface is wet, then tip off whatever pools in the bottom of the bowl.

04

Combine the sugar and the kosher salt, add to the nuts, and toss until the coating is even and matte.

~cinnamon-maple· step 04 instead

Combine the maple sugar, the cinnamon and the kosher salt, add to the nuts, and toss until the coating is even and matte.

05

Spread in a single layer, dot with the butter, and bake 40 minutes, stirring every 15.

~lavender-coconut· step 05 instead

Spread in a single layer, dot with the butter, and bake 30 minutes; scatter the coconut over the top for the last 10 so it toasts rather than burns.

06

Cool completely on the pan — the coating only sets on the way down to room temperature.

07

Break apart and finish with the flaky salt.

Four variations branch off this method. Each one rewrites at most one step; the other six are shared, so a fix to step 03 fixes it everywhere at once.

Section B

On the home grid

One tile, five photos. Hover a sliver to preview that branch; click it to open the recipe page on that variation.

the collage

The base photo keeps the top three-fifths of the 4:5 frame. The four variations run as equal slivers underneath, split by 2px paper gutters so they read as separate photographs, not one crop.

the indicator

Three signals, no colour: the split frame itself, a hairline chip reading "base + 4", and four short rules under the title — one per branch, the diff mark carried onto the grid.

in context

Ordinary tiles keep one uninterrupted photo and no chip, so the branched tile is legible at a glance without shouting.
