Branched recipes

Eight ways a base recipe could carry variations — Spiced Nuts with Cinnamon-Maple, Lavender-Coconut, Rosemary-Black Pepper, Chili-Lime branches. Each concept shows both halves of the problem: how branching reads on the recipe page, and how the variations collage into one tile on the home grid with a clear branch indicator.
