Spiced Nuts

35m  ·  serves 8  ·  easy  ·  approved  ·  via Family Cookbook

One egg white, one sheet pan, thirty-five minutes. This is the jar that sits out at Christmas and the base every other jar in this house branches off — baked until the coating goes matte and dry to the touch.

Method — tap a step to check it off

01

Heat the oven to 300°F and line a sheet pan with parchment.

02

Whisk the egg white with a splash of water until loose and foamy — not stiff, not meringue. It is glue, not structure.

03

Tip in the pecans and almonds and toss until every surface is wet, then tilt the bowl and pour off whatever pools at the bottom.

04

Add the sugar, salt and pepper. Toss until the coating is even and slightly tacky, then stir through the melted butter.

05

Spread in one layer and bake 35 minutes, stirring twice, until the coating is matte and dry to the touch. They crisp as they cool.

fig. A — recipe page, shelf selected: base the shelf scrolls sideways · 5 tiles, 4 fit

Why a shelf and not tabs: tabs make you choose before you have read anything, and they hide three of the four options. A shelf is a display case — the whole family is visible at a glance, in photographs, in the order the cook added them. It also already exists in this codebase: CompareShelf on /flavor is the same horizontal snap-scroller with the same rule-and-micro-label header, so nothing new has to be invented or learned.

The selected state carries no box. A 1px black rule under the chosen photo and an underline on its name — the two emphasis devices the system already owns. A hairline stem drops from the selected tile into the read-out below so the connection survives scrolling the shelf.

Three signals, in falling order of subtlety. (1) The composition itself — a wide panel with a stack of spines is unmistakable at grid scale even before you read a word. (2) The count chip, top-left, using the same white / hairline-border square as the card's own hover controls, with a 13px lucide branch glyph at strokeWidth 1.8. It sits left so it never collides with the status and heart controls that appear top-right on hover. (3) The tag line, which already carries "cuisine · signature" today and simply gains "· 4 variations" in the same lowercase micro-voice. Use the toggle above the grid to judge the tile with the chip, without it, and against how it looks today.

Cost of the composition: the base photo loses about a third of its width, so tightly-cropped food shots take a hit. Below three variations the spines get tall and the stack looks unbalanced; above five they get too thin to read. The honest range is 2–5 branches, with a "+N" spine if a recipe ever exceeds that.

Not built into this mockup: the editor flow for creating a branch, mobile behaviour of the shelf (it scrolls the same, but the read-out stacks to one column), and what happens to search — presumably a hit on "lavender" should surface the Spiced Nuts tile with the Lavender-Coconut spine promoted.
