Accordion Ladder

The base recipe is read once, in full. Underneath it the variations hang as a stacked list of collapsed rungs — name, one-line gloss, and a delta digest you can scan. Open a rung and you get only the delta: what is added, what is dropped, what is re-measured, and the two or three steps that actually change. The base never repeats itself. Built for the case where a recipe has grown eleven children.

One recipe, then a ladder of deltas. The page reads exactly as it does today — head, hero, lede, ingredients, method — and the branching arrives afterwards as a section, not as a mode. Every rung is one line high when shut, so eleven variations still fit on one screen. Opening a rung never re-prints the recipe: you see the ingredient rows that differ (added / dropped / re-measured) and the numbered steps that differ, keyed to the step numbers you just read. Try it: open a rung; switch opening between one at a time and many at once; hit expand all to feel how a long ladder behaves.

Spiced Nuts

35 min  ·  serves 8  ·  easy  ·  approved  ·  via Family Cookbook

base of a ladder · 7 variations below

Egg-white glazed nuts baked low and slow until the coating sets to a dry, brittle shell. The base is deliberately plain — sugar, salt, a little cayenne — because it is the trunk everything else grows off.

Method — tap a step to check it off

01

Heat the oven to 300F and line a sheet pan with parchment.

02

Whisk the egg white with the water to a loose foam — slack, not peaked.

03

Tip in the nuts and turn them through the foam until every surface is wet.

04

Stir the sugar, salt and cayenne together, then fold through the nuts with the melted butter and vanilla.

05

Spread in a single layer. Bake 30 minutes, stirring once at the halfway mark.

06

Break the sheet apart while still warm. Cool completely before it goes in a jar.

Notes & trade-offs

concept 07

What it is good at. Scale. Seven rungs cost seven lines; twenty would cost twenty and still be scannable, because every rung carries the same five facts — number, name, gloss, delta digest, share-of-recipe meter. Nothing is duplicated anywhere on the page, so a change to the base propagates to every variation for free. The delta digest (+2 −1 · 2 steps) plus the ink meter lets you tell a seasoning tweak from a near-rewrite without opening anything.

What it costs. You can never read one variation as a continuous recipe — the delta view assumes you have the base in your head or on the screen above. That is fine for a cook who owns the base recipe and wrong for a guest following a link. The Open as its own page action is the escape hatch, and it is a second view to build. Deltas against a sibling rather than the base (rung 06 branched off rung 01) also need a rule about which parent you diff against; the mockup defaults to the base and offers the sibling diff as a toggle.

Fidelity to the system. No new colour, no radius, no shadow, no filled badges. The rung head reuses the method-step grid (26px number column, zero-padded, tabular), the delta ingredient rows reuse the ingredient-row idiom including the struck-through style already used for checked-off ingredients, and the meter uses the Flavor Lab ink #141310 on the #eee empty track. Expansion is instant rather than animated, per the colour/opacity-only transition rule.
