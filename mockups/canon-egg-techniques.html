Canon · branching on technique

Every egg, one technique apart

Twelve dishes out of one egg. Almost nothing is added — what separates them is what state the egg is in when it meets heat, and what you do to it after that.

The dimensions

State — in its shell, cracked out whole, or beaten. This is the first real fork and everything downstream depends on it. Added — usually nothing; the handful that do take something take very little. Method — one chip per move, so “fried in fat” and “flipped” stay separate things you either did or didn’t. Doneness — where you stopped, which is the whole difference between over easy and over hard.
