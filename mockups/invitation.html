Nothing to fix yet.

The loop is the feature. The invitation asks the one question that otherwise gets asked across a table with the food already on it, and the answer lands where it can do something — in the check that knows Mira can't eat four of these courses. Everything else on the page is there so people actually reach the question: the menu, because it is the reason to come; the shape of the evening, because "seven courses" and "drinks and a bite" are different nights and guests dress for the wrong one; and the calendar button, because an invitation nobody diarises is a text you have to send again on Friday.
