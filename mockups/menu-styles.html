Twelve menu styles

One card, twelve typographic identities — pick by the vibe of the night rather than by what the app happens to look like. Under this there is a single component and twelve sets of custom properties, so a thirteenth is about ten lines and none of them can drift apart.

The styles

Same menu in all twelve. Only the type changes — face, case, tracking, alignment, the rule, and how much air sits between courses.

The paper under it

A second axis, independent of the type: leave it plain, or put it on a stock. These preview as the printed card rather than as a page element, so a tinted sheet stays tinted in dark mode instead of pretending to be a dark card.

On cardstock. The print rules here set one card per sheet at A5 with a 24mm head margin, which is the size that reads at a place setting and trims out of an A4 sheet with one cut. Anything heavier than about 250gsm needs the plain or cream stock — the laid and deckle treatments are printed texture, and on a paper that already has tooth they fight it.

What is actually here. Twelve style classes, six stocks, one card component and a print stylesheet. The two axes are independent — any style on any stock is 72 combinations without writing any of them. Try File → Print to see the trim.
