Where crème brûlée stops being crème brûlée

Each step states what the dish does at that moment, in the order you do it. Keep going down the spine and you arrive at the dish. Step off at any point and you have made something else — and that something else has a name, a recipe, and a tree of its own. Nothing here is a failure; the branches are just other desserts.

Why this shape

Every leaf is another dish’s tree. Panna cotta is a dead end here, but it has its own page where the spine reads “set with gelatin” and crème brûlée is the branch. The whole canon is one graph, entered from whichever dish you happened to look up.

The path is the definition. Read only the boxes down the middle and you have the essence — a baked custard, set by egg, burnt sugar on top. The branches are what makes it legible: you understand a dish by what it is not quite.

Your own recipe walks the path. A branch of your crème brûlée that swaps vanilla for cardamom never leaves the spine, so it is still the dish. One that sets with gelatin lands on panna cotta — and the app can tell you so, because it knows which fork you took.
