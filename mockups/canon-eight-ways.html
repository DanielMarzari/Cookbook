Eight ways to define a dish

The same gazpacho in each, stripped to what actually holds it together: a one-line essence, then the conditions that have to be true. What you may swap sits quietly alongside. Where a broken condition lands you on a dish that already has a name, it says so — and where it doesn't, it stays silent rather than inventing one.

01

Numbered conditions

The plainest reading. Order implies sequence, which is true here — you cannot body a soup you have not built.

Gazpacho

A raw vegetable, seasoned and bodied, poured cold.

  1. 1Raw fruit or vegetable
    tomato, melon, corn, beet, almond & grape
  2. 2Garlic, olive oil, acid, salt
    sherry, wine, citrus, verjus
  3. 3Bulked to a pourable body
    bread, almonds, its own starch
  4. 4Blended, served cold
02

Checklist

Reads as a test you run against a dish in front of you. Good if you ever want it interactive — tick the boxes and find out what you actually made.

Gazpacho

A raw vegetable, seasoned and bodied, poured cold.

  • It is raw — tomato, melon, corn, beet
  • Garlic, olive oil, acid, salt — sherry, wine, citrus
  • It pours — bread, almonds, own starch
  • It is cold
03

One sentence

The least furniture of any option: the definition simply is a sentence, with the load-bearing words marked. Hardest to scan, best to read.

Gazpacho

A raw fruit or vegetable, seasoned with garlic, olive oil, acid and salt, bulked until it pours, and served cold. Everything else — which vegetable, which acid, what does the bulking — is yours.

05

Must / may

States the whole idea in two column headings. The most compact way to show that the fixed and the free run in parallel.

06

Labelled rows

Each condition gets a name you can say out loud — the one that survives being quoted back. Matches how the recipe pages already label things.

Gazpacho

A raw vegetable, seasoned and bodied, poured cold.

RawAn uncooked fruit or vegetable
tomato, melon, corn, beet
SeasonedGarlic, olive oil, acid, salt
sherry, wine, citrus, verjus
BodiedBulked until it pours
bread, almonds, own starch
ColdBlended, and served cold
07

Index card

Small enough to sit beside a recipe rather than on its own page. The whole definition in about thirty words.

Gazpacho

Raw · seasoned with garlic, oil, acid and salt · bulked until it pours · served cold.

Swap the vegetable, the acid, the thickener. Serve it hot and it is a stew.

08

With the exits marked

Keeps the neighbouring-dish idea, but only on the conditions where breaking one lands somewhere that already has a name. Three of four stay silent.

The same idea, another dish

Ice cream is the clean case for marking exits, because both exits are dishes people already order by name. In 06, it reads:

Ice cream

A sweetened dairy base, churned while it freezes.

BaseDairy, sweetened
cream, milk, or both
CustardEgg yolks, cooked to a custard
no eggs → Philadelphia-style
ChurnedAgitated while freezing, so air enters
unchurned → semifreddo
Fat & airRich, and generously aerated
less fat, less air, warmer → gelato
