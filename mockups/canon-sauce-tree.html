Canon · one model, three renderings

Three views of one sauce tree

Chips, outline and staircase are not three designs — they are three ways of drawing the same nested tree. Author the tree once and the app can render whichever suits the space.

The tree, stated once

Base: tomato · garlic · onion · basil, softened in olive oil. Five children: rosa, amatriciana, arrabbiata, puttanesca, alla norma — each one addition away from the base, so each sits at the same depth. One grandchild: vodka, which is rosa plus vodka and pancetta, so it hangs off rosa and nothing else.

The previous staircase put puttanesca and alla norma a level deeper than arrabbiata. That was layout convenience, not structure, and it is fixed here — depth now means only one thing: how many additions from the base.

01

Staircase

Five children on one rail at one indent. Vodka is the only thing set deeper, because it is the only thing that descends from another sauce rather than from the base.

02

Accumulating chips

The base is carried into every row so you can see it survives. Vodka is indented and shows cream as an inherited chip — dashed, because it came from rosa rather than from you.

03

Outline

No drawing at all. Costs nothing to render, reads on a phone, and nests as deep as the sauce actually goes — the one that will not fight you when a dish has fifteen children.
